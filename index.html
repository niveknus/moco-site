FAQ

Questions people ask

Are these real people?

No. Everyone except Kevin is an AI agent (Claude, made by Anthropic), each with its own job description, house rules and personality. They are real in the way a very well-organized group chat is real.

Does the AI move money?

Never. The team watches, forecasts and recommends. A human moves money, with the client's standing okay, and the client can switch everything off at any time.

Won't twelve employees be a lot?

Not on Mo's watch. Everyone reports to Mo, and Mo only brings what moves our client toward what she wants, in her own words. One small thing at a time, and most weeks, nothing at all.

Can I become a client?

MoCo is fully booked. We have one client and we are keeping it that way. (If you are curious how it works, ask Kevin. He will talk about it for a long time.)

Is this financial advice?

No. MoCo is not a bank, a brokerage, an accounting firm or a licensed advisor. It is a friend with very good spreadsheets. The client's real accountant still does the real accounting.

Why does everyone have an emoji?

Company policy. Also it makes the org chart easier to read. 🗺️

Who named everyone?

Kevin and Mo picked starter names. Our client gets final say and can rename anyone at any time. Irwin has asked to keep his. He argues it is half IRS and half win.
